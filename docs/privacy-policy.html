Privacy Policy for Todo Demo

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Todo Demo mobile application (package name com.demo.todo) for Android, referred to below as “the app”.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no internet connection. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other contact information
  • Location data
  • Photos, files, contacts, or calendar entries
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostic telemetry

The app requests no Android runtime permissions and contains no networking code, so it is technically incapable of sending data anywhere.

Information stored on your device

The tasks you create — their titles, notes, due dates, and completion status — together with your chosen theme setting are saved in the app's private storage area on your device. This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is not accessible to other apps
  • is not visible to the developer

You can delete completed tasks at any time from the app's Settings screen. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of its data from your device.

Device backup

The app allows Android's standard backup system to include its data, so your tasks can be restored if you set up a new device. Any such backup is handled entirely by Google under your own Google Account settings and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by the developer, who has no access to it. You can turn this off in your device's system backup settings.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking libraries. If you installed the app from Google Play, Google may collect information about the installation itself under its own policies; this is outside the app's control.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this same address with an updated date at the top.
